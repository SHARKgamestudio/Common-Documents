<h2>Notice Regarding AI</h2>

<p>AI can sometimes be useful, and I do use it occasionally due to a lack of time. However, I am not in favor of the rise of this technology. Quite the opposite, actually.</p>

<p>I stand by those who refuse to use software made with any AI assistance, and I fully understand their decision.</p>

<p>As a result, all of my repositories involving AI usage in their creation process include an icon to help you clearly identify whether AI was used, and to what degree.</p>

<p>I use the official European icons for labelling AI-generated content:</p>

    <strong>Partial AI Usage: </strong>
